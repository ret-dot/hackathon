Attack lab
Fraud is stopped by signatures, counters, quotas and caps, not by blocking the queue.






Replay is caught by the nonce. A forged quantity breaks the signature. A rolled-back counter breaks monotonic order. The double-dip is flagged because both offline shops see the same quota. The per-card quota bounds how much any offline shop can issue, and the signed lot cap bounds the shop (try 60+ kg).
